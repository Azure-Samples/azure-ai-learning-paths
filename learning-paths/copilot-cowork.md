## Readiness check

Create a low-risk sample workflow and explain:

- The work delegated to Cowork and the expected output.
- The rules, skills, or plugins involved.
- The permissions and data boundaries.
- How a user reviews the result before taking consequential action.
- The operational and usage-cost considerations.

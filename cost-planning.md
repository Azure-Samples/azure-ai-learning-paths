## Estimation exercise

For a sample workload, record:

- Number of users and expected interactions.
- Average interaction complexity and connected capabilities.
- Model, storage, search, networking, and monitoring assumptions.
- Development, test, and production environments.
- Expected growth and peak usage.
- Known exclusions and uncertainty.

Estimator output is guidance only. It is not a binding offer, availability guarantee, or substitute for current product documentation and professional pricing guidance.

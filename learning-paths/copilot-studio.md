## Practice exercise

Design a sample agent and document:

1. The selected harness and selection criteria.
2. Knowledge sources, tools, and authorization boundaries.
3. Evaluation and monitoring approach.
4. Environment and application lifecycle strategy.
5. Estimated usage and the assumptions used in the estimate.

Estimator results are guidance only and are not binding prices or availability guarantees.
